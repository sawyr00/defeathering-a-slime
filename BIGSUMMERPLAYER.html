The Beach Slime media player skin. Disfiguring The Goddess's Big Slime Summer album artwork illustrated by Karmavoid is actualized into a 3D visual piece with integrated music player functions. With multiple functions including rotations, visualizers, skin modes, and other bonus features, this player incorporates the brutality of DTG's themed compilation of tracks combined with a fun, laid-back, enjoyable summer beach vacation. This is a standalone player and not a "skin" in the traditional sense, as it cannot be applied towards WMP or Winamp applications.

BEACH SLIME PLAYER is a Windows desktop music-player skin built in Unreal Engine. It runs as a downloadable Windows application.

Recommended: Windows 10/11 64-bit, quad-core Intel or AMD processor, DirectX 11 compatible graphics card, 8 GB graphics memory, 16-32 GB system RAM, 2.75 GB of harddrive space, and current graphics drivers.
